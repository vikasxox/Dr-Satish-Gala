DEPLOYMENT
1. Upload this whole folder to a static host such as Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages or Hostinger.
2. Keep the images folder beside index.html. Do not move the nested media and reviews folders.
3. No build step is required. This is a vanilla HTML/CSS/JS site.
4. The Google Maps button uses the exact Maps link supplied for the clinic in the project brief.

CONTENT NOTES
The supplied clinic banner is used as the top identity artwork. The supplied portrait is used as the primary doctor photograph. The supplied reception and consultation photographs are used in the clinic section. The supplied clinical photograph is shown as clinical media with context.
All 25 supplied review screenshots are included in images/reviews and rendered in the review wall. Tap any review to open the full screenshot with previous and next navigation.
Clinic hours on the page follow the supplied clinic branding: Monday to Saturday 6:00 PM to 9:15 PM, with 9:15 AM to 10:00 AM consultations by appointment. Sunday is shown as closed.
The mobile phone number comes from the supplied clinic information. The banner also shows the landline 26341414.

MEDICAL CONTENT
Testimonials are presented as individual patient experiences, not as verified clinical outcomes or promises. The site also carries a discreet urgent care note in the footer.
